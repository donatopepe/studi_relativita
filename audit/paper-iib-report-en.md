# Paper II-B — English audit of fixed barrier

## UMCH-P2B-0001 — Model and status

Unchanged model: `L=mc[-1+ε/(κ/κ₀-1)]`, `κ>κ₀`, `ε>0`. UMCH status: `UNPROVEN`. Model B state: `CONTRADICTED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS`. Paper III gate: `BLOCKED`.

## UMCH-P2B-0002 — Provenance

General `L(κ)` EOM/invariants from Arreaga–Capovilla–Guven `hep-th/0105040`, E42/E45/E46. Momenta, Hessian, and constraints from Capovilla–Guven–Rojas `hep-th/0111014`, E19/E24–E31. Reproducible algebraic specialization is not independent proof of formalism.

## UMCH-P2B-0003 — Legendre and Hessian

On domain `Lκ<0`, `Lκκ>0`: curvature Legendre map is invertible. Sourced Hessian has only tangent null direction in generic sector. This enables canonical analysis but proves neither bounded energy nor stability.

## UMCH-P2B-0004 — Canonical structure

Sourced specialization: normal highest-derivative momentum, conserved Poincaré momentum, primary `P·Xdot=0`, secondary `Hc=0`, no other constraints, and two first-class constraints in generic sector; source count `2N`. This is not an independent bracket derivation.

## UMCH-P2B-0005 — Stationary sector

Planar sector has unique physical root `z*=1+ε+√(ε²+ε)>1`. Linearization: `δz''-(κ₀z*)²δz=0`. A growing local planar mode exists. This is not a full constrained instability proof.

## UMCH-P2B-0006 — Standard limit

`κ₀→0` limit is nonuniform: barrier vanishes at fixed positive κ, stays finite along boundary/stationary paths, and geodesic κ=0 remains outside domain for every positive κ₀. Mode timescale grows, but solution-space convergence is not established.

## UMCH-P2B-0007 — Observable

No experiment→κ₀ mapping is derived. Momenta and constraints alone provide no measurement protocol.

## UMCH-P2B-0008 — Decision

Model fails preregistered gates: reduced local stability, standard full-solution-space limit, observable mapping. Decision `CONTRADICTED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS`; Paper III `BLOCKED`. This is not a full falsification of UMCH or other barriers.

## UMCH-P2B-0009 — Unresolved limits

Bounded reduced Hamiltonian, full nonlinear/constrained stability, and causality remain unresolved. They are neither fabricated nor used as decision basis.
