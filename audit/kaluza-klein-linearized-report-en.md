## Model and primary record

The bounded toy background is `M5=R1,3 x S1`, with `y~y+2*pi*L`. The sourced static scalar potential is differentiated to form the project weak-field tidal Hessian `T_ij=partial_i partial_j Phi`. Full raw matrices, radial/transverse eigenvalues, source/probe circle profiles, three-dimensional source profile, and finite-window geometry are retained. The Hessian is not a complete gauge-fixed five-dimensional tensor perturbation.

Canonical scope: `Liu2003CompactifiedPotential` supports the exact compact-circle point potential; `FloratosLeontaris1999` supports the KK sum and short/long limits; `KehagiasSfetsos2000` supports KK eigenmodes and leading Yukawa range/degeneracy. None establishes this finite-window protocol, identifiability, `ell0`, UMCH, or detection.
