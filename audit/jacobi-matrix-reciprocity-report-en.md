# Matrix Jacobi reciprocity audit

- Classification: `EXACT_MATRIX_JACOBI_CONTROL_AND_NEGATIVE_RESULT`
- Status: `JACOBI_MATRIX_PROFILE_REVERSAL_TRANSPOSE_RECIPROCITY_SINGULAR_VALUES_BLIND_NOT_ELL0`
- Detection: `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`

For tested exact two-, three-, and four-segment symmetric optical profiles, reversing full profile transposes vertex Jacobi block. Therefore singular values, determinant and Frobenius norm remain reversal-blind; only oriented block entries may differ after screen frames and transport are fixed. This is a deterministic piecewise-matrix control, not a universal smooth-profile theorem or exact-spacetime derivation. `ell0` is absent.
