# Jacobi reversal-involution audit

- Classification: `PROJECT_DERIVATION_AND_EXACT_MATRIX_CONTROL`
- Status: `JACOBI_FINITE_SYMMETRIC_PROFILE_REVERSAL_EXACT_BLOCK_TRANSPOSE_RECIPROCITY_NOT_ELL0`
- Detection: `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`

For exact finite products of symmetric optical segments, anti-involution `R(P)=E P^T E` fixes every segment and reverses product order. Thus reversed vertex block is exactly transpose of forward block for any finite segment count. Singular values, determinant and Frobenius norm are reversal-blind; oriented antisymmetric component changes sign in fixed endpoint frames. This is not a smooth Sachs theorem or exact-spacetime result. `ell0` is absent.
