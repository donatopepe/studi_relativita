\begin{abstract}
We study as a falsifiable hypothesis the existence of a universal scale $\kappa_0>0$ with inverse-length dimension. This draft reports neither a detection nor a complete theory. The source audit supports, under conditions, timelike Frenet--Serret kinematics, but does not derive the positive bound. With $\kappa=\sqrt{a^\mu a_\mu}/c^2$, a timelike geodesic has $\kappa=0$; the postulate is therefore in tension with ideal free fall until a new covariant dynamics compatible with experiment is supplied. Postulate status: \texttt{UNPROVEN}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definitions and domain}\label{sec:definitions}
Let $x^\mu(\tau)$ be a regular timelike curve in a Lorentzian manifold with signature $(-,+,+,+)$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:velocity}
u^\mu=\frac{dx^\mu}{d\tau},\qquad u^\mu u_\mu=-c^2,
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:acceleration}
a^\mu=u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu.
\end{equation}
When $a^\mu\ne0$, define proper worldline curvature and normal by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:curvature}
\kappa=\frac{\sqrt{a^\mu a_\mu}}{c^2},\qquad N^\mu=\frac{a^\mu}{c^2\kappa}.
\end{equation}
This quantity is not identified with intrinsic spacetime curvature. Null curves, fields, and vacuum are outside this definition.

\section{UMCH hypothesis}\label{sec:hypothesis}
The core conjectures
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hypothesis}
\kappa\ge\kappa_0>0,\qquad [\kappa_0]=L^{-1}.
\end{equation}
$\kappa_0$ remains a free parameter. Relations to $c,\hbar,G,\Lambda$, or cosmological scales are outside the core without an independent derivation. UMCH-CLM-0093 remains \texttt{UNPROVEN}.

\section{Minimal kinematic derivation}\label{sec:derivation}
Metric compatibility gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:orthogonality}
\frac{D}{d\tau}(u^\mu u_\mu)=2u_\mu a^\mu=0.
\end{equation}
For $\kappa\ne0$, the definitions give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:frenet}
a^\mu=c^2\kappa N^\mu,\qquad N^\mu N_\mu=1.
\end{equation}
This is a definitional identity, not a proof of inequality \eqref{eq:hypothesis}. For $P^\mu=m_0u^\mu$ with constant mass,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:momentum}
P^\mu P_\mu=-m_0^2c^2.
\end{equation}
Orthogonality preserves the local norm of $u^\mu$; alone it does not imply global energy-momentum conservation.

\section{Tension with geodesic motion}\label{sec:tension}
A proper-time-parametrized timelike geodesic obeys $a^\mu=0$. Equation \eqref{eq:curvature} then gives $\kappa=0$, while $N^\mu$ is undetermined. Thus \eqref{eq:hypothesis} excludes an ideal geodesic. A UMCH theory must introduce a new action or equations, demonstrate the standard limit, and confront equivalence and Lorentz tests \cite{Will2014}. Nonzero spacetime curvature does not resolve the tension: curved spacetimes admit geodesics.

\section{Required limits}\label{sec:limits}
As $\kappa_0\to0$, a proposed deformation must recover inertial motion in special relativity, geodesic fall in general relativity, and the corresponding conservation principles. Null, weak-field, and nonrelativistic limits require explicit treatment. A singular limit must be derived rather than asserted.

\section{Falsification criteria}\label{sec:falsification}
The timelike formulation is rejected or restricted if no covariant dynamics exists; unavoidable instability or acausality appears; every $\kappa_0>0$ is excluded; the standard limit fails; or no distinct observable content results. Upper bounds precede any positive measurement. Without a derived map between an experimental observable and $\kappa_0$, reporting a numerical bound is not justified.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}
This paper does not yet define null, field, or vacuum sectors; propose an action; prove stability; treat ALD, QED, quantum gravity, or cosmology; or measure $\kappa_0$. The bibliography is preliminary. Unaudited claims are excluded from results.
