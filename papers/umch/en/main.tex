\begin{abstract}
We reorganize the Universal Minimum Curvature Hypothesis (UMCH) from its initial worldline premise to the multiscale operational formulation. A geodesic particle may have $a^\mu=0$ in curved spacetime; hence the previous pointwise core is \verb|SUPERSEDED_AS_CORE| and fixed models A/B are \verb|CONTRADICTED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS| within scope. The new core uses finite regions, a physical frame, a raw response vector, and the Minkowski null model. We prove that $\ell_0$ is \verb|ELL0_STRUCTURALLY_NON_IDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_CURRENT_FAMILIES|. A turnover family makes $\ell_0$ identifiable in principle if shape is fixed independently, but remains \verb|MATHEMATICAL_IDENTIFIABILITY_CANDIDATE_ONLY|: local geometry justifies $p=2$ only for area-normalized channels and does not derive exponential coefficient $q$. Overall status: \verb|UNPROVEN| and \verb|NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM|.
\end{abstract}
