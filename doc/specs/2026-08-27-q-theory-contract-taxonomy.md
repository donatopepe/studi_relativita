# Exhaustive scenario taxonomy for an independent q theory contract

## Scope

Systematically classify broad mechanism scenarios that could generate exponential/turnover behavior. Exhaustive means exhaustive by mechanism class under declared criteria, not proof over every imaginable future theory.

## Acceptance contract

A q-producing theory must specify: covariant action/operator; degrees of freedom; independent parameters/scales; state and boundary data; causal prescription; map to one preregistered raw response channel; exact derivation of asymptotic profile; dimensionless q; no free scale degeneracy; GR/Minkowski/nearly-flat limits; stability/unitarity/ghost conditions where relevant; predictions beyond turnover; falsifiers.

## Scenario classes

- mass gap/massive propagation;
- spectral gap/transfer operator;
- compactification/Kaluza-Klein tower;
- screening/environmental effective mass;
- local higher-derivative poles;
- infinite-derivative entire form factors;
- covariant perturbative/heat-kernel form factors;
- causal-set nonlocal d'Alembertian;
- asymptotic-safety effective form factors;
- stochastic/noise-kernel correlation scales;
- cosmological horizon/curvature radius;
- topology/boundary eigenmodes;
- critical/RG correlation length;
- fractional operators (often power-law tails);
- quasinormal/resonance decay (time/ringdown, not generic spatial scale response).

## Decision rule

Reject as q derivation if it introduces an unrelated free length/mass, depends on environment/state/boundary without unique prescription, yields wrong functional variable/asymptotic family, or lacks channel mapping. Passing mechanism-class plausibility is not adoption.

Expected literature conclusion: no model-independent q; viable future contract most naturally requires a dimensionless eigenvalue fixed by a fully specified covariant operator plus boundary/state data, and an independently fixed relation between its physical correlation length and ell0.
