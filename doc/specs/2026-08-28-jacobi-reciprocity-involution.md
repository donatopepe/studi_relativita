# Matrix Jacobi reversal involution derivation

## Goal

Replace finite numerical pattern with exact algebraic derivation for any finite product of symmetric piecewise-constant optical segments. Determine which full-map and vertex observables are forced to be reversal-blind.

## Alternatives

1. Add more random segment tests: useful stress test but never proves finite-product identity.
2. Derive adjoint involution for exact segment maps (selected): bounded, algebraically closed, and directly explains prior failures.
3. Claim smooth reciprocity theorem: rejected because smooth covariant screen transport and endpoint conventions are not derived here.

## Proposed identity

Let `P=[[A,B],[C,D]]` be a `4x4` phase-space map in displacement/velocity blocks. Define

`R(P)=J P^T J`, with `J=diag(I,-I)`.

For each exact segment generated by symmetric `K`, test algebraically/numerically that `R(P)=P`. Since `R(XY)=R(Y)R(X)`, reversed product equals `R(P_forward)`. Therefore its blocks obey

- `A_rev=A_fwd^T`;
- `B_rev=-B_fwd^T` under this candidate sign convention;
- `C_rev=-C_fwd^T`;
- `D_rev=D_fwd^T`.

The sign convention must be tested against actual propagator and corrected rather than forced. A different fixed block involution may be required.

## Tests

- identify exact involution satisfied by one segment;
- prove anti-automorphism numerically for generic matrices;
- apply to two/three/four-segment products;
- recover observed `B_rev=B_fwd^T`;
- enumerate invariant scalarizations: singular values, determinant up to parity, Frobenius norm;
- show raw oriented antisymmetric component changes;
- preserve `ell0` absence and scope.

## Scope

`PROJECT_DERIVATION_AND_EXACT_MATRIX_CONTROL`. Finite products only. No claim for arbitrary smooth Sachs systems, exact spacetime, physical detector calibration, or UMCH. Structural dead-end criteria fail because smooth connection-derived profiles and physical channel maps remain open.
