# Independent theoretical mechanisms for turnover q

## Question

Can canonical existing theory independently fix the dimensionless `q` in `F_T=A x^p exp[-q(x-1)]` without using UMCH turnover data or introducing a degenerate free scale?

## Candidate classes

1. Massive-field/massive-gravity propagation: exponential/Yukawa range is controlled by a mass scale `m`, so decay rate is `m` (in natural units) and matching gives `q=m ell0`. Unless `m` is independently measured and tied to ell0 by theory, this adds a second scale and does not fix q.
2. Infinite-derivative/nonlocal form factors: covariant functions of `Box/M_s^2` introduce a nonlocal scale `M_s`. Their position-space profile is not generically the project radial exponential. Matching may give q as a function of `M_s ell0` and form-factor choice; without independent action/scale it is model-dependent.
3. Covariant perturbation/heat-kernel form factors: nonlocal curvature form factors are standard, but depend on operator spectrum, state, boundary conditions, and masses. They establish admissible mathematical structures, not universal project q.

## Decision

Canonical literature provides independent mechanism classes but no model-independent dimensionless q for UMCH. Each class introduces scale/operator/boundary input. Therefore:

`NO_MODEL_INDEPENDENT_Q_DERIVATION_FOUND`

and current gate remains `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`.

This is literature-scope result, not proof impossible in every future theory. No candidate is adopted.
