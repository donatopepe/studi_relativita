# English glossary

## UMCH-TERM-0001 — Spacetime curvature
Intrinsic curvature of Lorentzian geometry, described by the Riemann tensor; it is not trajectory curvature.

## UMCH-TERM-0002 — Geodesic curvature
Covariant measure of a curve's departure from geodesic transport under a declared connection and parametrization.

## UMCH-TERM-0003 — Proper acceleration
Acceleration measured by an accelerometer comoving with a timelike worldline.

## UMCH-TERM-0004 — Timelike curve
Curve with timelike tangent that admits proper time.

## UMCH-TERM-0005 — Null curve
Curve with null tangent and no proper-time parametrization.

## UMCH-TERM-0006 — Field observable
Gauge-invariant local or relational quantity constructed from fields and geometry.

## UMCH-TERM-0007 — Vacuum observable
Operationally defined quantity associated with a geometric vacuum solution or quantum state.

## UMCH-TERM-0008 — κ₀
Hypothetical universal parameter with dimension `L⁻¹`; it remains free and unmeasured in the core.

## UMCH-TERM-0009 — Falsifiability
Ability to specify observations or inconsistencies that would reject a hypothesis.
