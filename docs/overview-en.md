# UMCH overview — English

## UMCH-OV-0001 — Question

Can a universal constant `κ₀>0`, with dimension `L⁻¹`, be associated with a lower bound on physically observable curvature? Status: `UNPROVEN`.

## UMCH-OV-0002 — What is not assumed

Spacetime curvature, worldline curvature, proper acceleration, field observables, and vacuum observables are not synonyms. AI output is not evidence. No starter source proves `κ₀>0`.

## UMCH-OV-0003 — Foundational result

For a regular timelike curve, `κ=√(a^μa_μ)/c²` is a consistent conditional definition. If `a^μ≠0`, `a^μ=c²κN^μ`; this does not imply a positive bound. An ideal geodesic has `a^μ=0` and `κ=0`.

## UMCH-OV-0004 — Main tension

`κ≥κ₀>0` excludes timelike geodesic motion in the current formulation. A new covariant dynamics with standard limit, conservation, stability, and experimental comparison is required.

## UMCH-OV-0005 — First experimental study

Outcome `NO_BOUND_DERIVABLE`: free-fall data do not automatically become a bound on `κ₀`. No derived mapping yet connects an experimental observable to a universal proper acceleration predicted by UMCH.

## UMCH-OV-0006 — Sector status

Null curves, fields, and vacuum remain open problems. ALD, QED, gravity, and cosmology are not foundational-paper results and must depend on audited claims.
