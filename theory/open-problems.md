# Open problems

1. Does a covariant positive lower bound on timelike proper acceleration admit geodesic free fall or require violation/modification of equivalence principles?
2. Can a local action impose a lower rather than upper curvature bound without instability, nonlocality, or extra unphysical modes?
3. What parametrization-independent null observable could carry the same `κ₀` scale?
4. Which gauge-invariant field observable has inverse-length dimension and operational meaning?
5. Can a vacuum-sector observable be universal across inequivalent states and backgrounds?
6. Does the `κ₀ → 0` limit recover each standard theory continuously?
7. Which existing experiment yields a model-independent upper bound rather than a bound conditional on new dynamics?
8. Are claims about ALD runaway removal consequences of a derived equation or imposed trajectories?
9. Do Kaluza–Klein, QED, gravitation, and cosmology modules add independent predictions?
10. Is UMCH observationally distinct from known acceleration scales, modified inertia, curvature-squared dynamics, or cosmological parameters?
