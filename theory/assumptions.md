# Assumptions

## Core hypothesis

`UMCH-A-001`: There may exist a universal constant `κ₀ > 0` with inverse-length dimension. This is falsifiable and currently `UNPROVEN`.

`UMCH-A-002`: In the theoretical core, `κ₀` is a free parameter. Relations to `c`, `ħ`, `G`, `Λ`, or cosmological scales are exploratory conjectures.

`UMCH-A-003`: Every claimed sectoral bound must use a covariant, operationally meaningful observable and state its parametrization behavior.

`UMCH-A-004`: The standard theory must be recovered through a controlled `κ₀ → 0` limit wherever UMCH claims a deformation.

## Not assumed

- Existing spacetime curvature does not prove `κ₀ > 0`.
- Coordinate bending does not prove physical curve curvature.
- Timelike proper acceleration does not define null, field, or vacuum curvature.
- Dimensional analysis does not derive a value.
- Removal of runaway solutions, quantization, gravitation, or cosmological consequences are not granted by the core hypothesis.

## Scientific burden

A model must state action or equations, degrees of freedom, constraints, conserved quantities, causal structure, initial/boundary data, and observables. Existing data may force `κ₀ = 0` or exclude a proposed sectoral realization.
