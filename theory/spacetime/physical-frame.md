# Physical frame

Use unique regular future timelike eigenvector of `T^μ_ν` when available: `MATTER_FRAME_RESOLVED`.

In vacuum/degenerate sectors, `CMB_CONTINUATION_RESOLVED` requires a preregistered certificate containing boundary/anchor hypersurface, future-directed unit timelike anchor field, covariant transport rule, admissible path/domain family, uniqueness within declared tolerance, and causal/domain coverage of the target. The continuation is boundary data; it is not inferred from local vacuum curvature alone.

Missing anchor/transport/path declaration, competing admissible paths, or insufficient coverage gives `FRAME_UNRESOLVED` with a reason code. Such region never counts as confirmation and forces `ell0` to `NON_IDENTIFIABLE`. Even a resolved frame is necessary but not sufficient for identifiability or a positive-floor claim.

Preferred realized-state frame does not itself prove Lorentz violation of local laws. Schwarzschild without cosmological boundary data stays unresolved; a certified vacuum toy tests only the gate contract.
