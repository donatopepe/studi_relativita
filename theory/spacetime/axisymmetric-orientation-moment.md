# Axisymmetric orientation second-moment gate

Classification: `PROJECT_DERIVATION_AND_NEGATIVE_RESULT`.

Status: `AXISYMMETRIC_ORIENTATION_AVERAGE_SECOND_MOMENT_ONLY_NOT_ELL0`; `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`.

For `E(n)=I-3nn^T` and any normalized orientation measure invariant under rotations about z, define `mu2=<cos^2 theta>`. Axisymmetry gives

`<nn^T>=diag((1-mu2)/2,(1-mu2)/2,mu2)`,

hence exactly

`<E>=diag((3mu2-1)/2,(3mu2-1)/2,1-3mu2)`.

Every nonzero average lies on one of two opposite signed axial rays. At `mu2=1/3`, average vanishes; crossing this moment reverses signed ray. Distinct measures with same `mu2` are exactly indistinguishable under averaged operator: higher moments and detailed angular profile are in null space of this statistic.

Thus measure family is not identifiable from this operator average, and zero/sign reversal can be moved by orientation second moment. Unsigned projectivization erases reversal entirely. Fixed covariant measure gives geometric orientation moment only; `ell0` is absent.

This theorem extends uniform-cap control but still assumes common tangent-space alignment and omits Schwarzschild parallel transport and radial amplitude variation. Covariant spacetime measure/transport remains open; no core reformulation is triggered.
