# Operator-native exact controls

Status: `PROJECTIVE_SCALE_NON_IDENTIFIABLE_IN_CURRENT_EXACT_CONTROLS`; `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`.

In declared orthonormal frames, retain dimensionless tidal/magnetic eigenvalue tuples, rank, trace, Frobenius norm, multiplicity, and projective spectrum.

- Minkowski: zero endomorphisms, rank zero, projective spectrum undefined.
- FLRW: isotropic triple-degenerate tidal spectrum; direction fixed under ell^2 rescaling.
- Schwarzschild: projective tidal spectrum proportional to `(-2,1,1)`; anisotropy distinguishes geometry pattern but not scale.
- VSI type-N toy: nonzero electric/magnetic operator spectra despite vanishing polynomial scalar invariants; fixed toy polarization pattern.

At fixed local curvature, changing ell multiplies each operator by ell^2, so trajectory is radial and projective spectrum constant. This exact-control family cannot identify ell0. Noncollinear scale evolution needs curvature variation across windows, transport/boundary effects, cross-channel structure, or another physically derived landmark. Such structure is not inferred from these controls.
