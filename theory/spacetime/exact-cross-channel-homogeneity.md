`y_1/y_2=(g_1/g_2)(c_1/c_2)(k_1/k_2)`,

so operational scale cancels. If gains, channel normalizations and geometric amplitudes are independently fixed, ratio recovers only geometric/channel amplitude relation. If gain or geometry ratio is free, they are multiplicatively confounded. A zero denominator channel makes ratio undefined.

Thus combining exact channels does not generate scale information merely because records are multidimensional. Unequal exponents would create algebraic scale dependence, but no such physical exponents or UMCH law are derived here.

This is algebraic specialization of existing exact patterns, not new GR or observational result. Open routes remain; no core reformulation is triggered.
