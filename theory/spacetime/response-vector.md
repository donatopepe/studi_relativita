# Operational response vector

Preserve raw vector

`R=(R_tidal,R_mag,R_hol,R_clock,R_null,R_cong)`.

Candidate dimensionless responses include `R_tidal=ℓ² RMS(E_μν)`, `R_mag=ℓ² RMS(B_μν)`, normalized finite-loop holonomy, differential clock response, null-bundle distortion, and congruence deformation.

Preregister norms `C_2=||R||_2` and `C_infinity=||R||_∞`. Never switch norm after data. Raw vector remains public. Exact loop/kernel/subtraction/uncertainty protocols are required before observational use.
