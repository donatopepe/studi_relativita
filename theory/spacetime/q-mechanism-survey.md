# Independent q-mechanism survey

Status: `NO_MODEL_INDEPENDENT_Q_DERIVATION_FOUND`; gate `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`; `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`.

Canonical mechanism classes were checked:

1. Massive propagation (`Hinterbichler2012`) supplies a mass/range. Matching an exponential gives, schematically in natural units, `q=m ell0`. If `m` is free or unrelated to `ell0`, second-scale degeneracy remains.
2. Infinite-derivative/nonlocal gravity (`BiswasEtAl2012`) supplies covariant form factors and a nonlocal scale, but not automatically project radial `F_T`; `q` depends on action, scale, and mapping.
3. Covariant curvature form factors (`BarvinskyVilkovisky1990`) depend on operators, spectrum, state, boundaries, and masses; no universal scalar q follows.

These sources establish legitimate theory classes, not UMCH. No candidate yields model-independent dimensionless q from current assumptions. Adopting one would change theory contract and needs human ratification plus independent tests.
