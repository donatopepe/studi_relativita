`P(lambda,L)=[[cos(wL),sin(wL)/w],[-w sin(wL),cos(wL)]]`, `w=sqrt(lambda)`.

For two distinct segments, products `P2P1` and `P1P2` generally differ but are similar because `P1(P2P1)P1^-1=P1P2`. They therefore have identical determinant, trace, eigenvalues and characteristic polynomial. Total conjugacy spectrum cannot identify cyclic order.

Vertex source `(d0,v0)=(0,1)` therefore gives identical observer displacement `d(S)=M01` under reversal, despite different full maps. Observer derivative `d'(S)=M11` generally differs. General displacement with `d0!=0` can also differ. Thus whether order is observable depends on retained phase-space component and boundary phase; displacement-only vertex channel has larger null space than full Jacobi map.

Identical segments and special phases erase even full-map order difference. Affine normalization, source/observer boundary, screen transport and measured phase-space components must be fixed.
