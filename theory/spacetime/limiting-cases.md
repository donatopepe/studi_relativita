# Limiting cases

- Minkowski: raw response exactly zero.
- Nearly flat family: responses may tend continuously to zero mathematically.
- FLRW: cosmological frame and Ricci/expansion responses.
- Schwarzschild vacuum: Ricci zero but Weyl/tidal response nonzero; frame continuation declared.
- VSI wave: polynomial scalar invariants may vanish while operational tidal/magnetic responses persist.

Exact cases validate definitions, not positive floor in realized universe.
