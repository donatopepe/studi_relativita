# Operational response protocols

Status: definitions/protocol gates, not detection.

## Holonomy

Preregister finite loop shape/orientation and area bivector. Leading small-loop proxy `R_hol=||R·Σ||`; for characteristic area `ell²`, toy scalar proxy is `ell²|curvature_scale|`. Higher-order corrections and loop optimization are excluded until derived.

## Clock network

`R_clock=|Δτ_residual|/τ_ref` after preregistered flat kinematic baseline, worldlines, synchronization, and reference duration. Gravitational/kinematic subtraction cannot be selected after data.

## Null bundle

`R_null` is normalized Jacobi-map/shear/convergence departure at fixed affine/source-observer geometry. Affine normalization, caustics, screen basis, and flat comparator are required.

## Congruence

`R_cong` is normalized expansion/shear/vorticity departure from matched flat congruence with same initial data. Congruence selection is preregistered.

## Frame

`MATTER_FRAME_RESOLVED` for unique timelike Tmunu eigenvector; `CMB_CONTINUATION_RESOLVED` for unique preregistered vacuum continuation; otherwise `FRAME_UNRESOLVED`, never confirmation.

## ell0 identifiability

Requires resolved frame, fixed protocol, fixed norm/family, likelihood/uncertainty model, and bounded nuisance degeneracies. Current state `NON_IDENTIFIABLE`; no real data or ell0 inference.
