`BA=A^{-1}(AB)A`,

so cyclic re-anchoring yields similar matrices with identical trace, determinant and eigenvalues. Raw finite-loop matrix difference can therefore encode base-point/frame representation rather than conjugacy-invariant shape.

Singular values are not invariant under arbitrary similarity; they are meaningful only after metric/frame convention and allowed gauge group are fixed.
