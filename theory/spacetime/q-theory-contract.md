# Theory contract for an independent q

Status: `NO_MODEL_INDEPENDENT_Q_DERIVATION_FOUND`; no scenario adopted.

A valid extension must provide all of:

1. covariant action/operator and causal prescription;
2. degrees of freedom and symmetries;
3. independent parameters/scales with no free-scale degeneracy;
4. state/boundary data and uniqueness domain;
5. stability, regularity, and ghost/tachyon/unitarity conditions as applicable;
6. derivation of position/scale asymptotic, not assumed `F_T`;
7. explicit channel map into one preregistered raw response component;
8. a dimensionless q fixed before UMCH data;
9. GR, Minkowski, nearly-flat, and declared boundary limits;
10. predictions beyond turnover and independent falsifiers.

Surveyed mechanism classes: mass and spectral gaps, compactification, screening, higher-derivative poles, entire and covariant form factors, causal-set nonlocality, asymptotic safety, stochastic correlations, cosmological horizons, boundary/topology spectra, critical/RG correlations, fractional operators, and quasinormal resonances.

Every class currently fails at least one contract item: second scale, environment/state/boundary dependence, wrong observable/variable/profile, operator/action freedom, or absent channel map. Most promising mathematical route is a dimensionless eigenvalue of a fully fixed covariant operator, but its physical correlation length must still be independently related to `ell0`; saying so is a design criterion, not a derivation.

Gate remains `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`; `NO_POSITIVE_DETECTION_CLAIM`.
