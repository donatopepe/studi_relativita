# English report — free-fall identifiability

## Outcome

`NO_BOUND_DERIVABLE`

Under the audited timelike definition,

\[
\kappa = a/c^2,
\]

an upper bound on the relevant residual proper acceleration `a` would, under a declared direct mapping, imply `κ₀ ≤ a_upper/c²`. Reviewed sources describe curvature formalism and equivalence tests, but the UMCH core supplies no equations identifying an experimental sensitivity with that universal `a`.

## Why no number is reported

Free-fall tests measure quantities defined by their experimental models, often differential accelerations or violation parameters. UMCH does not specify direction, phase, composition dependence, state dependence, effective noise, or coupling of its proposed deviation. Directly substituting a published precision would add a new unverified assumption.

Therefore `κ₀` is not identifiable from these sources using the kinematic core alone. A covariant dynamics predicting the observable is required first. This result is neither a measurement of `κ₀=0` nor evidence for `κ₀>0`.
